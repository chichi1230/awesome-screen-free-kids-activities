# Awesome Screen-Free Kids Activities

A small curated list of printable, hands-on, and low-prep activities for kids.

This list is meant for quiet time, homeschool breaks, classroom centers, rainy days,
travel folders, and simple family activities that do not need another app or video.

## Art and Coloring

- [Crayola Free Coloring Pages](https://www.crayola.com/free-coloring-pages) - Printable coloring pages, activity sheets, and seasonal art pages.
- [SuperColoring](https://www.supercoloring.com/coloring-pages) - Large collection of coloring pages across animals, holidays, places, and more.
- [Monday Mandala](https://mondaymandala.com/) - Printable coloring pages, craft templates, and activity sheets.
- [Just Color](https://www.justcolor.net/kids/) - Coloring pages for kids, with animals, cartoons, holidays, and nature themes.
- [ColorKind](https://colorkind.app/) - Turns ideas or family photos into printable coloring pages, with five free creations for new installations.
- [Huebloom](https://huebloom.art/) - Free printable coloring pages for kids, classrooms, and screen-free art time.
- [Huebloom Learning Coloring Pages](https://huebloom.art/coloring-pages/learning) - Alphabet and number coloring pages for early learning.

## Learning Printables

- [K5 Learning Free Worksheets](https://www.k5learning.com/free-worksheets-for-kids) - Printable math, reading, vocabulary, and early learning worksheets.
- [Math Salamanders](https://www.math-salamanders.com/) - Printable math worksheets, puzzles, and practice pages.
- [Education.com Worksheets](https://www.education.com/resources/worksheets/) - Printable worksheets by grade, subject, and skill.
- [Reading Rockets Literacy at Home](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home) - Reading tips, family literacy ideas, and hands-on learning resources.
- [NASA For Kids and Students](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/for-kids-and-students/) - Space learning activities, printables, and student resources.
- [Scholastic Learn at Home](https://classroommagazines.scholastic.com/support/learnathome.html) - Reading and learning activities for different grade levels.

## Crafts and Making

- [FirstPalette](https://www.firstpalette.com/) - Kids crafts, templates, coloring pages, and printable projects.
- [DLTK's Crafts for Kids](https://www.dltk-kids.com/) - Crafts, coloring pages, worksheets, and themed printables.
- [Activity Village](https://www.activityvillage.co.uk/) - Coloring pages, puzzles, crafts, and printable activities for kids.
- [Red Ted Art](https://www.redtedart.com/) - Paper crafts, seasonal projects, and simple making ideas.
- [The Craft Train](https://www.thecrafttrain.com/) - Kids craft ideas with many printable templates.

## Puzzles and Brain Games

- [Puzzles to Print](https://www.puzzles-to-print.com/) - Printable word searches, mazes, crosswords, and logic puzzles.
- [KrazyDad Puzzles](https://krazydad.com/) - Printable sudoku, mazes, kakuro, and other pencil puzzles.
- [Activity Village Puzzles](https://www.activityvillage.co.uk/puzzles) - Printable puzzles for different ages and themes.
- [Math Is Fun Worksheets](https://www.mathsisfun.com/worksheets/) - Printable math worksheets and practice pages.
- [Puzzle Baron Printables](https://www.printable-puzzles.com/) - Printable logic puzzles, crosswords, and word games.

## Calm and Mindful Activities

- [Mindful - Mindfulness for Kids](https://www.mindful.org/mindfulness-for-kids/) - Simple mindfulness ideas and family-friendly practices.
- [Positive Psychology Kids Worksheets](https://positivepsychology.com/mindfulness-for-kids/) - Mindfulness activities and worksheets for children.

## Nature and Outdoor Ideas

- [National Wildlife Federation Kids](https://www.nwf.org/Kids-and-Family) - Outdoor activity ideas, wildlife learning, and family nature projects.
- [The Nature Conservancy Nature Lab](https://www.nature.org/en-us/about-us/who-we-are/how-we-work/youth-engagement/nature-lab/) - Nature learning resources and activities for students.
- [National Geographic Kids](https://kids.nationalgeographic.com/) - Animal facts, nature ideas, and kid-friendly science topics.

## Notes

- Some resources are fully free, while others include both free and paid materials.
- Always check each site's terms before using materials for commercial projects.
- This list is independent and not affiliated with the sites listed above.

## Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. Please prefer resources that are:

- useful for parents, teachers, homeschoolers, or librarians
- printable, hands-on, or easy to use away from a screen
- kid-friendly and reasonably easy to access
- not mostly ads, popups, affiliate pages, or paid bundles
